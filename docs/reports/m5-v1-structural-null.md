# Memory v1 retirement: observed null and non-decision-bearing passability

Research allocation decision, 4 October 2026, base `ee6bf61`. Memory v1 is retired
from further collection under the capability-first phase. This does not complete
its 48-cell matrix or held-out evaluation, and does not qualify R2. R2 remains
open for a decision-bearing v2 study.

## What was observed

The [44-cell readout](m5-development-readout-44.md) retains 44/48 accepted cells,
396 condition readouts and 352 own-versus-reference pairs. All task comparisons
tie; 340 goal comparisons tie and own is one tick later in 12. All 120 applicable
stale-correction comparisons tie across 15 change cells. All 15 null cells retain
false targeting, while 14 no-event cells have zero selector calls/behavior bytes.
Four C3 cells retain broader state differences; 40 later cells have strict core
forks. Shared namespaces and repeated conditions are not independent samples.

The remaining-07 failure is retained and no partial cell is promoted. Its
completion amendment and the v1 held-out outline are not executed. Their old
reports/protocols remain unchanged. Historical mechanism, replay and frozen-input
motor-sensitivity checks remain valid within their declared scopes.

## Structural limitation and uncertainty

`memory_task.advance_memory` selects active inspection versus the common-goal
bearing. `memory.select` uses cue/relation associations for targeting. No
operational passage-route choice consumes M's passability estimate. Estimates
still matter to evidence assimilation, conflicts, evaluator gold, validation and
inspection resolution; saying that nothing reads M would be incorrect.

The ordinary route often supplies the changed passage observation without a
targeted detour. U can nevertheless change requests, bearings and neural actions.
Thus the current experiment offers little demonstrated opportunity for useful
passability-based route choice, but equal outcomes on future runs are not
mathematically forced. No held-out inference, significance claim, biological
memory claim or guarantee about different geometry follows.

## Why held-out was declined

The [v1 outline](../plans/m5-heldout-proposal.md) projected approximately 7.4-8.1h
and 16.3GB extra retained output. These are historical planning estimates, not
current guarantees. The user selected bounded research exploration and retired
v1: further confirmation of this narrow profile is a lower priority than a scene
where remembered passability actually affects a decision. A rigorous null can be
scientifically useful; this allocation decision does not manufacture completion.

The [new phase](../plans/research-capability-phase.md) prepares a v2 design with
paired route decisions, explicit null costs, actual sensing and unchanged honest
failure accounting. No v2 implementation or reserved-data access occurs here.
