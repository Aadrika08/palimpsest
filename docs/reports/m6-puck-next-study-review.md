Balanced aggregate and approach-turn joint MSE worsen, as does turn heading MAE. Training turn joint MSE improves while training velocity leakage worsens. This rejects the specific equal-mass pilot as a repair, not every conceivable imbalance effect. [Original residuals:51](m6-puck-student-diagnosis.md#L51); [balanced comparison:16](m6-puck-balanced-residual-v1.md#L16).

Their BEFORE-approach contact/ready-but-not-arrived joint states were absent from teacher training support (0 teacher rows versus 1,575 student rows). This is a measured coverage gap and plausible compounding-error mechanism, not proof that collecting recovery examples would fix control. Existing training-row errors mean distribution shift cannot be the sole explanation. [Diagnosis:10–39](m6-puck-student-diagnosis.md#L10).

**What the representation does and does not explain.** [project:286](../../src/palimpsest/puck_profile.py#L286) supplies egocentric puck/goal positions divided by 14, puck velocity divided by 2, and two contact flags. [prepare:65](../../src/palimpsest/puck_learning.py#L65) reconstructs float64 geometry from float32 sensors, stages the sensor-only phase transition, but deliberately gives the model the BEFORE-mode bit. That bit resolves the historical missing carried-state distinction. The action still depends on geometry and the staged transition; it is not a lookup from that bit alone.

[demonstrate:376](../../src/palimpsest/puck_profile.py#L376) combines goal-distance normalization, axial/lateral tests, a behind-point target, phase switching, wrapped heading, nearest forward/reverse orientation and clipping of the heading command. Velocity jumps between zero and ±.25 or +.2 at alignment/phase/stop boundaries. Nearest-orientation switching can also reverse the turn direction. The [finite 9→32→32→2 tanh network:77](../../src/palimpsest/puck_model.py#L77) is smooth for fixed mode/contact bits. It cannot exactly represent a discontinuity over a continuous domain, but that does **not** prove insufficient capacity on this finite dataset, nor that smoother approximations must fail the task. Conditioning, optimization, geometry representation and label discontinuity remain hypotheses. More width, a new geometry feature or an analytic zero-velocity gate would each change the scientific contract; none is established as the smallest fix. Gating the model’s action analytically would also change its learned-action responsibility.

**Selection is correct but is not a task metric.** [fit:149–163](../../scripts/train_puck.py#L149) evaluates unweighted dev loss and saves the earliest strict minimum for both objectives, exactly as declared. Dev has 811 positive-push rows versus 106 approach-turn rows; each row has equal selection weight, although rare severe errors can still dominate loss contribution. This proxy does not require successful alignment or goals. There is no retained per-epoch stratum comparison here proving a different epoch solves the failure. Changing selection now, extending epochs or choosing a seed would be a new intervention, not correction of a violated protocol. [Balanced selection:60](../plans/m6-puck-balanced-loss.md#L60).
