## Concrete decision blockers

1. **No actual compact acceptance yet.** Execution-audit trust set (`memory_case_execution_pins_v1.py`) and condition qualification tuple (`memory_condition_qualification_pins_v1.py`) remain empty. Prospective protocol claims are false. Source tests cannot fill these anchors. This review neither supplies an audit receipt nor authorizes allocation, native launch or pruning.
2. **Irrecoverable fields require an explicit evidence decision before deletion.** Full arbitrary-tick pixels, population statistics and latency vectors are not retained. Existing row/camera hashes authenticate commitments, not recoverable values. A non-destructive comparison can establish conversion fidelity and attest original checks; it cannot make discarded bytes independently inspectable afterward. Full perception, arbitrary-image, population or timing claims require additional retention if needed by the paper.
3. **Endpoint sufficiency must be demonstrated, not inferred from schema.** The smallest preservation check must compare unchanged raw and compact derivations of all registered analysis cells, family contrasts, guards and dispositions, plus snapshot/event/source/model binding and no-event full-raw equality, under the admitted contract. Include natural failures, failures before terminal publication and corruption refusals. Retain both forms throughout the check; a passing fixture is only software evidence, with actual admitted-run preservation still separate.
4. **A case check is not the full study gate.** Future execution still needs complete development acquisition and precision gate, separate held-out freeze/draw/access, all scientific/observer gates, budget/retention admission for both blocks and human acceptance. Do not rewrite these requirements around a convenient compact file set.

A preservation-check proposal should name its source SHA, exact inputs and hashes, retained output inventory, comparator/figure scripts, complete accounting and success/failure criteria before execution. Its verdict must separately state (a) exact reproducibility from retained bytes, (b) pre-prune attestations only, (c) absent capabilities, and (d) unresolved paper figure requirements. **No deletion follows automatically from passing that check.**
