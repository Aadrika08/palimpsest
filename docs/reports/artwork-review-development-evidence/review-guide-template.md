## Suggested review: at most ten minutes

Use one minute for orientation, one minute per mode, two minutes for notes and
one minute to close. This is a review cap, not a record of a performed session.
Begin audio at low volume, start it manually, and use the player's stop control.
Allow at most **two labelled plays**: mark the first play and any second play.
There is no autoplay. Do not stretch, normalize or loop the choir.

Record reviewer, machine/viewport, source/software/artifact hashes, actual start
and end, interruptions and any help. For each mode choose **keep**, **revise
presentation** or **insufficient sample**, with one reason. Audio can be marked
not reviewed. Missing judgment does not count as acceptance.

This review can inform presentation choices and identify insufficient material.
It cannot qualify sustained artistic quality, scientific effects, independent
installation/operation, exhibition duration or physical safety. Those remain open
and require their own complete sessions and declarations.
