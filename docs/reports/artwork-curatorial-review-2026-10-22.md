# Agent curatorial review — 22 October 2026

## Recommendation

Build the encounter around **one interruption, two traces**: let a viewer see
what the controller received, what changed, and what it actually did afterwards.
That is the strongest connection between the [PRD's central product loop](../borrowed-body-prd-v1.md)
and the [brainstorm's proposed first encounter](../borrowed-body-brainstorm.md).
The six exports can support that encounter, but the present route pair does not
yet carry it. My recommendation is to retain this package as a development study
and revise the central comparison before presenting it as a finished artwork.

This is an agent's recommendation requested by the user, not independent human
acceptance. I inspected the retained Afterimage, route, Atlas and specimen
screenshots and read the source reports and intent documents. I did not spend
ten minutes viewing each work, listen to Choir, or operate an exhibition setup.
Technical results below come from the linked reports; curatorial judgments are
interpretations, not measured audience responses.

Reviewed package: `runs/static-artwork-sessions-2026-10-22-v1/encounter/index.html`,
as recorded at commit `ab28e7f`. No artwork bytes or mappings were changed by

## Order of work

1. **Resolve One map's source adequacy.** Before generating anything, declare one
   supported intervention, snapshot boundary, unchanged control continuation,
   edited continuation, duration/storage ceilings and stopping rules. Prefer
   the documented obstacle-relocation encounter if supported by the current
   public path. Use a common post-snapshot comparison window and label natural
   early termination. Retain the current short pair as evidence. Do not keep
   rerunning until routes separate. Exit: a viewer can identify the shared
   state, single edit and actual continuation of both conditions, including
   an honestly reported overlap or null result.
2. **Make Afterimage's transformation legible.** Improve the explanation and
   source-to-response association without altering the accepted mapping. Exit:
   at desktop and mobile sizes, the viewer can identify the source, current
   response and distinct source/model times. A small response is a valid result.
3. **Assemble a short guided encounter.** Lead with the route comparison and its
   Borrowed body trace; offer Afterimage and Choir as other transformations of
   records, then Atlas and the specimen pair as structural studies. Use the
   brainstorm's two-to-three-minute introduction as guidance, while preserving
   each declared artifact duration. Avoid implying all six share one controller
   or one recording. Exit: the introduction explains what changed and offers
   inspection without requiring readers to work through the audit reports.
4. **Resolve Choir listening and presentation level.** Keep technical export
   acceptance closed, but conduct actual listening on the intended device and
   record its setup/level, audibility and fatigue. Its reported peak is
   −31.813 dBFS and RMS −40.088 dBFS. Do not silently normalize or change the
   fixed mapping to compensate. Exit: a listener gives a concrete keep/revise
   decision and a usable playback-level policy.
5. **Complete the independent handoff and use declarations.** An independent
   operator opens the package and finds sources, limits and controls. The owner
   records audience/use, permissions and custody/retention. These remain real
   human and ownership decisions; an agent recommendation cannot fill them in.

The first blocker is **a readable intervention comparison**, not a new model,
hardware deployment or a positive memory result. This review does not reopen
retired research allocations or change scientific release gates.
