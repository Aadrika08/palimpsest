# One new actuator-response body: prospective implementation plan

**Goal:** one fixed left/right actuator gear `[2.1, 3.0]` body, fresh bounded wheel calibration, then frozen-fit encoder homing on the known route. **Status:** plan only; no source implementation, controls or execution approved by this document. Root selected this case after the bounded source review. Base: `ebb7352c99f75fb36be52c9f7294aa0b077b2ff8`.

**Architecture:** a named standalone driver uses manual/reproduction Engine ownership and recording, explicit checked world/body authoring, existing pure fit/control functions and a private encoder task. No new Engine/profile/snapshot dispatch, controller registry or UI. **Stack:** existing Python, NumPy, MuJoCo/EGL and repository helpers; no dependencies. **Spec:** [approved M6 scope](../implementation-plan.md), [PRD body/home requirements](../borrowed-body-prd-v1.md), and the exact [protocol JSON](m6-new-body-response-protocol.json), SHA256 `8ea826bf4981d3db70bada1ea88db9a0d8707a3a9433e7c247e4254ec7f24672`.

This is adaptation with permitted measurements on a previously unevaluated actuator-response case, **not zero-shot transfer**. It cannot establish a body population, unseen route, task-level compensation benefit, learned policy, physical capability or full M6. Existing [calibration](../reports/m6-body-adaptation-v2.md), [turn transfer](../reports/m6-body-turn-transfer.md), [two changed task starts](../reports/m6-task-heldout-starts.md), and [homing composition/UI](../reports/m6-body-homing-composition-v2.md) retain their original scopes; no repeated browser/replay campaign is included.

## Model, state and publication admission

Before every episode's first action, require paused/zero-time/zero-current-tick state, zero wheel angles/qvel/controls/manual intent/speed/stall, no pending commands, and declared pose/world/calibration. Before first gear assignment validate the nominal authored model hash already pinned in `body_profile.ARTIFACT`. Compare canonical model values: after the declared lane authoring, only `actuator_gear[left_motor,0]` may differ. Bind the resulting full hash and require it unchanged at all five initials and during measurement. Authoring itself allows only named obstacle joint ranges/positions, goal XY and fresh pose/cache resets; not a blanket model exclusion.

The fixed artifact binds protocol/source/model, producer run, four exact episode/tick ranges, sample order/hash, all trial fits and pooled fit/hash. Homing re-admits it before first action and checks immutable hashes afterward. It never restores calibration native bytes. The initial SNPs retain actual model/native/RNG state but do not supply missing study-state restore semantics.

Reuse the instance-local one-row deferral already present in `collect_trial`: join Engine data with the complete 10-pre-control/last-cache contact packet and private study arrays, then publish exactly once. Restore writer method and callback in `finally`; retain any rejected pending interval without counting it as accepted. No global monkeypatch. Contacts remain sampled/cache evidence, not continuous collision or physical-rest proof.

## Bounds, capacity and dispositions

Two owners, at most **1,080 intervals**, five initial SNPs ≤16 MiB each, 256 MiB recording/64 MiB result per owner, 128 KiB fit artifact, 1 MiB report/log stream, **2 GiB total and 1 GiB free floor**. Admission totals **644,317,184 bytes (614.46875 MiB)**: 252,149,760 numeric (1,080×233,472), 80 MiB SNP, 128 MiB results, 6 MiB logs, 24 MiB controls, 128 MiB staging and 8 MiB headroom. The existing 32-KiB added-field/chunk allowance must be proved against the actual future schema before controls approval; exceedance rejects, never expands the cap.

Mandatory **fresh whole-study capacity check** before any owner: both rows and every artifact allowance, managed quota, exclusive absent output, and free disk ≥2 GiB cap +1 GiB floor. Recheck before each owner/reset and final publication. One inner writer lease; no outer lease. Sampling/writer checks are not atomic filesystem reservation. Do not substitute a stale planning-time disk count.

Preserve 180 s/owner, 780 s internal and **830 s normal invocation-through-exit admission**. Later separately reviewed controls must terminate the full invocation at 840 s, KILL at 843 s, supervisor fallback 846 s, then at most 10 s failure cleanup; drains are bounded to 2 s/stream. These later failure bounds do not extend normal acceptance or promise universal kernel return. Reuse finite first-sample-ready monitoring (0.25 s, ≤64/sample, ≤128 cumulative PID/start, ≤8192 `/proc` scan), 60 s startup/30 s progress checks, exact identity rechecks and existing TERM/KILL/drain helpers. Any forced cleanup, survivor, monitor error or late disposition fails positive evidence.

Always retain both planned rows. Distinguish operational completion from functional acceptance: rejected calibration blocks homing; valid unsuccessful homing is a negative functional result; protocol/infrastructure failure stops immediately and retains remaining `not_started` rows. No retries, alternate body, new fit, deadline extension or threshold relaxation. Final success requires both functional gates **and** source/storage/timing/cleanup admission. Full producer/upstream/runtime/assets/controls are rehashed before/after even on failure.

Estimated native wall time **3–8 minutes**, not measured or guaranteed: prior adaptive 2,276-row gate took 251.7 s; composed 853-row gate took 361.4 s. Different ownership/state work prevents a defensible constant rows/s estimate. A timeout remains a retained failure. No neural fitting or physical hardware is proposed.
