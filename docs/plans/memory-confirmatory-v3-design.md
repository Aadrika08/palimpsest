## Evidence and question

acquisitions and 24 continuations: 22 goals, mandatory checks passed, and
`mechanism_observed=False`. Own/sham local-null terminated with `CueUnavailable`,
scored 900 each, and travelled less distance before failing than no-U. Preserve
those outcomes and the frozen path-based predicate; do not regrade the pilot.
Its correlated cases supply neither independent-family variance nor a sample size.
The selected-profile prerequisites remain valid only within their recorded scope;
legacy raw restore inequality and unsupported full-prefix policy replay remain.

Question: under one fixed symbolic-memory routing policy and CPU FLYNN controller,
does historical U change goal-reaching cost, including natural failures, across
a prospectively defined distribution of unfamiliar simulated scene families?
This tests symbolic transfer and closed-loop consequences, not neural memory
learning, biological experience, arbitrary-world competence or physical safety.

The separate 300-second offline visibility diagnostic may attribute the recorded
null failure. It supplies no new route-feasibility trial, revised pilot result or
automatic permission to change scientific behavior. An unresolved finding remains
unresolved. The [v2 design's prerequisites](m5-v2-decision-scene.md#separate-pilot-and-stop-rules)
are not silently removed by calling the eventual result a null.

The [completed terminal attribution](../reports/memory-terminal-attribution.md)

## Conditions, resources and common fork

**Choice:** retain target limit one and the existing nine definitions, in this
registered order. A family-specific fixed condition permutation may be introduced
only prospectively if execution-order confounding is addressed in the protocol.

| Condition | Behavior resource and interpretation |
|---|---|
| own | U fitted only from the matching frozen historical E |
| swapped | U from the other frozen relation history |
| sham | Byte-identical own U; exact trace-invariance control |
| no-U | Empty U; no directed invalidation |
| hand-authored | Existing fixed `local_cue` rule, explicitly not learned |
| history-independent | Existing bounded fact-order selector |
| traversal | Existing cue-association traversal selector |
| retrieval | Matching historical E through the existing retrieval rule |
| oracle | Independently proved gold target IDs only; labelled privileged |

Reuse [`selection_for_condition`](../../src/palimpsest/memory_experiment.py),
[`condition_u`/`selector_config`/`score_nine`](../../scripts/verify_memory_transfer.py)
and [`memory_task.selector_resources`](../../src/palimpsest/memory_task.py).
Only retrieval gets historical E at behavior time; only oracle gets gold.
Report payload, envelope, validation, behavior and history bytes separately;
retrieval is not an equal-memory treatment. Five broader baselines are not all
U-only interventions. No condition gets privileged steering, pose or goal bearing.

Each case starts with unknown M and a fresh CPU FLYNN acquisition of both real
open observations, then a pre-event snapshot. All nine conditions fork its exact
bytes. Preserve native/model/RNG/world/M/E/detector/request/supervisor/command state
except declared U/selector/resource substitution and existing recorder provenance.
Retain [`condition_state`](../../scripts/memory_decision_pilot.py)'s exclusions;
no new normalization to make mismatched forks agree. Acquisition cost is separate.

Gold is evaluator-only: compare the source's actually observed expectations with
independently reconstructed pre/post passage occupancy from retained world geometry
and the actual applied intervention. A regime name or U prediction is not gold.
Null/no-event gold is empty. Oracle receives IDs plus an audited proof binding its
source snapshot, observations, geometry, event and hashes; other selectors never
receive that proof. A rejected closure is an invalid scheduled intervention, not a
successful change case. No-event must have zero selector invocations/behavior bytes;
ordinary route-required inspections may still occur and are reported separately.

## Independent families and required counterbalancing

**Fact:** the current [`make_case`](../../src/palimpsest/memory_decision_world.py)
couples preferred route, lexical `a`, learned relation and closure. Mirroring
changes side; its seed does not independently vary those scene dimensions.
Repeating that generator with new IDs cannot satisfy the required counterbalance.

**Choice:** a family is one independently generated admissible base scene and
controller-seed block, with matched change, cue-change/no-closure null, and
no-event cases. These three variants, all condition branches, mirror/lexical
variants and ticks are dependent members of that family, never extra samples.
Use separate fresh acquisitions per case until a separately reviewed source-fork
contract supports anything else. Three cases therefore require 3 acquisitions
and 27 continuations per family before any additional prerequisite owners.

The new generator must independently specify and retain:

- Learned relation and its mapping to physical north/south passage; change closure
  follows that mapping, never an incompatible regime/closure Cartesian product.
- Physical side of the lower public route cost; exact ties as a separate stratum.
  With a tie, the declared route-ID order determines the initial preference.
- Fact-ID ordering and subject/landmark lexical ordering, separated from physical
  side, route cost, association and route-ID tie breaking.
- Whether the changed passage is initially preferred or alternative, balanced
  through valid association/cost/ID assignments. Null/no-event twins retain them.
- At least two prospectively fixed admissible event schedules, after acquisition
  and before new passage evidence. Decision timing remains shared across conditions.
- Independently drawn admissible scene parameters beyond IDs: public waypoint/
  route placement and resulting lengths, with ranges fixed on development only.
  Preserve separator clearance, sensing contracts and common controller inputs.

**Choice:** use a balanced blocked assignment schedule and publish its complete
factor table and aliases before collection. Prefer a bounded balanced fraction to
an unaffordable full Cartesian product; do not claim separately identified factor
effects when interactions are aliased. Exact rows, parameter distribution and
number of independent families remain decisions before native entry. Pairwise
balance alone does not establish statistical independence or broad generalization.
Reject only prospectively specified pre-execution validity violations, retaining
every candidate ID/reason. Never reject a family because its controller failed or
because own U did not help. Native acquisition failure stops downstream conditions.

## Endpoints and analysis

**Choice:** keep 1,200 acquisition and 900 continuation ticks, .02-second controls,
the selected controller and existing sensory limits. The selected travel profile
is a baseline; new family geometry requires new source/profile identities and
affected feasibility checks, even if the controller is unchanged. Any further
scientific change needs its own evidence and versioned contract. Define the scored
origin as the common pre-event snapshot boundary used by `capped_score`, not each
branch's commitment time. Count actual issued continuation intervals.

For an independently audited natural terminal, score S = actual continuation ticks
if goal, otherwise 900. Early `CueUnavailable` therefore receives 900. An owner
wall timeout, missing proof or interrupted audit is incomplete infrastructure
evidence, not an invented task failure or score. Every registered disposition is
retained; missing mandatory cells prevent complete-study status.

**Choice:** two primary contrasts are own minus no-U and own minus swapped;
negative means lower cost. Within each family, average each contrast equally over
change/null/no-event, then average families equally. Report all three strata and
the fixed mixture; an overall gain cannot conceal null harm. Freeze the mixture
before collection rather than weighting it by realized success or cell survival.
Keep paired goal-success differences alongside capped cost to expose saturation.

Raw travelled distance is secondary: retain actual metres, actual duration and
terminal reason, including short paths ending in failure. Do not fill failure
paths with artificial distance or call a shorter failed path a benefit. Also
report blocked first commitment, inspections/time, replans, unavailable cues,
bypasses, event/commitment/sample boundaries and acquisition cost.

Keep the following endpoints distinct, with per-fact evidence and timestamps:

- Targeting: actual selected set, exact match, false/missed targets, abstention,
  precision/recall and own/swapped direction; empty denominators stay undefined.
- Uncertainty: invalidation, duration until fresh evidence and right-censoring.
  Marking an unchanged fact uncertain is not itself a false payload revision.
- Revisions: first correct observation versus first committed stale correction;
  unchanged-value confirmation separately; unsupported or incorrect payload/status
  revisions against actual evidence/gold counted as false revisions.
- Propagation: observed M/commitment inputs, chosen route, actual policy inputs,
  motor/action and trajectory differences, then task outcome. Difference is not
  benefit; goal failure is not erased by correct targeting.

Adapt v1's [`condition_readout`](../../scripts/verify_memory_counterbalance_comparison.py)
concepts, not its hardcoded tick boundaries. The existing
[`analyze_memory_matrix`](../../scripts/analyze_memory_matrix.py) accepts old
profiles/48 cells and cannot directly analyze this study.
**Choice:** control the two primary contrasts at familywise 5% using Bonferroni
(two 97.5% two-sided intervals); other contrasts are descriptive. Resampling, if
selected, must resample whole independent families within assignment strata.
The interval estimator, attainable family count, meaningful improvement threshold
and acceptable null-cost/success-loss margins require prospective justification
from new development evidence; this pilot cannot supply them. No significance,
equivalence or useful-null claim follows merely from failure to detect a benefit.

## Splits, gates and finite stages

Preserve the historical U/E inputs and provenance; no refitting in this proposal.
Training history, development and held-out must have disjoint family/entity/
evidence IDs and generation seeds, with explicit split validation/access logs.
Freeze generator/distribution, all source/runtime/profile identities, analysis,
thresholds and family count before assigning or opening held-out IDs. Do not
relabel v1 reserved seeds or development records as v3 held-out evidence.

1. **Protocol/software only:** zero native/model owners or scientific data reads.
   Implement the new generator/validator, nine-condition admission and gold proof,
   complete-slot readout and family analysis using existing helpers. Synthetic
   checks cover boundaries, resource isolation, deliberate invalid inputs,
   failure scoring and family grouping; independent review precedes registration.
2. **One new development phase:** only after an exact owner matrix and aggregate
   time/storage allocation are registered. Zero automatic retries, replacement
   families, fallback profiles or corrective studies. Qualify affected source/
   profile/snapshot/sensing paths; unchanged old prerequisites are not rerun for
   appearances, and changed scientific paths do not inherit their qualification.
   Collect all nine conditions and natural failures; review feasibility and
   variance before committing the confirmatory sample count and analysis.
3. **One held-out phase:** separately freeze/register only after mandatory
   development gates pass. Execute the entire predeclared matrix within its own
   finite allocation, without tuning, case substitution or post-result extensions.
   Independent close owns the final scientific result, not a preliminary driver.

Mandatory gates cover source/geometry/sensor validity, real acquisition, paired
invariants, oracle isolation, exact own/sham traces, decision opportunity, raw
audits, terminal proof, owner cleanup and complete matrix/analysis. Applicable
route/visibility feasibility obligations must also be satisfied before held-out.
Scientific outcomes are separate: benefit, harmful transfer or an adequately
precise null may all complete a technically valid study. Inconclusive precision,
missing cells or failed prerequisites remain explicit; tests cannot pass R2.

Before native entry decide: diagnostic implications and any source amendment;
the exact admissible family generator/balance table; the route-feasibility gate;
development family count; oracle proof construction; expected precision/effect
and null-harm margins; and finite preparation/owner/close/byte/cleanup caps with a
credible retention ledger. Reuse serialized CPU ownership, immutable inventories,
per-owner caps, original-clock deadlines and fail-closed storage admission.
No allocation is granted here. Preserve the old consumed allocations. Stop a
phase on invariant/resource/deadline/cleanup failure and retain partial evidence;
after its finite result, present the remaining decision rather than rescue until
success. R2 and the simulation release remain incomplete until their full gates pass.
